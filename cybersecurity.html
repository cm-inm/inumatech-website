Why offensive cybersecurity

Move from reacting to anticipating.

Reactive controls matter, but they do not show how an attacker might combine weaknesses. Offensive cybersecurity uses ethical attack techniques to test assumptions, expose credible paths, and help your team fix the issues that carry the greatest risk.
