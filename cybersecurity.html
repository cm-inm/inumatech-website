Authorized testing

Your scope. A closer look.

We look for the routes an attacker might take, but only in the systems you’ve approved for testing. Together, we agree on the boundaries before any work starts.

Why test

Beyond the warning light.

A scan can flag a problem. A penetration test goes further: can someone actually exploit it, what could they reach, and which fix should come first? That’s the picture we help your team build.
