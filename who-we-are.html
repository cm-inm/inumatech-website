WHO WE ARE

Built in Rwanda.

Inuma Technology is a Kigali-based technology company founded in 2017. We help organizations choose, build, secure, and support technology that solves real problems. Our work covers hardware, software, cybersecurity, multimedia, and long-term research. We use proven tools where they work and explore new ideas where they can make a practical difference.

Why Inuma.

We take time to understand the work before recommending the technology. That means fewer unnecessary products, clearer decisions, careful testing, and support that continues after delivery.

Vision

To build globally useful technology from Rwanda and show that strong ideas can come from anywhere.

Mission

To design, deliver, and support reliable technology that makes work simpler, safer, and more productive.

Practical technology, built and supported from Kigali.
