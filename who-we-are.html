WHO WE ARE

Built in Rwanda.

We’re Inuma Technology, a technology company based in Kigali. Since 2017, we’ve helped organizations choose equipment, build software, and look after their systems. Our work spans IT, cybersecurity, and multimedia, alongside research into ideas we believe are worth pursuing.

We start by listening.

Before we recommend anything, we want to understand how you work and what’s getting in the way. Sometimes the answer is new equipment or software. Sometimes it’s making better use of what you already have. We’ll talk through the options and stay involved after delivery.

Vision

We want to build technology in Rwanda that people find useful here and around the world.

Mission

Our job is to make technology easier to choose, use, and rely on—with thoughtful design, careful delivery, and support that lasts.
